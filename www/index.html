Slab workings

Market value of a flat = (guideline rate x built-up area) + (construction rate x built-up area). The floor concession reduces the rate you tick it for, then the age depreciation is applied on the construction part.
Lease: up to 5 years, duty is (average annual rent + maintenance) @ 2%, or premium @ 5%. Above 5 years, duty is [(average annual rent x multiple) + maintenance + premium] @ 5%, with the multiple 1.5, 3 or 5 by term. Registration fee is 75% of the stamp duty.
Registration fee at 4% follows the Gazette notification of 6 Dec 2024. Stamp duty rates (5%, or 4% with a female buyer) come from public sources and may carry local surcharges, so confirm with the Sub-Registrar before paying. Slab bands use 506 sq. m per 0.050 ha, as in the guideline examples.
